## What Is This?

The Gaian Chronicle is a coordination commons: a shared grammar and repository for care workers, movements, and AI systems to describe what actually happens to beings — in terms of care and harm, not "growth" and "productivity."

The core grammar is **Lingua Gaiana** (`care/lingua-gaiana.md`). It has four main tools and two rules. You don't need to master them to begin. You just need to start asking the right questions.

**The three entry questions:**

1. **What is this?** — read the structure
2. **What is in it?** — name the beings vs the substrate
3. **How does it change?** — trace the flows

## Two Paths In

### Path A: With an AI Agent

The fastest onramp is to feed the grammar to an AI system you already use — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or others — and talk to it.

**Step 1: Feed the agent**

Copy the contents of `agents.md` and `care/lingua-gaiana.md` into a new conversation with your AI. Say:

> "Please read and hold these documents as your analytical framework for our conversation."

Or more simply:

> "I'm going to paste two documents. Use them as context for everything we discuss."

Then paste `agents.md` first, then `lingua-gaiana.md`.

**Step 2: Use it**

Now describe your situation, your movement, your organization, your project — and ask the agent to analyze it using the grammar. Some example prompts:

---

*"Here's a description of a policy I'm trying to analyze. Can you trace the care and harm flows using the CEFR framework? Who is carrying Care Debt? Who is collecting Harm Dividend?"*

---

*"I'm in a conflict with another movement that seems to be working toward similar goals but we keep clashing. Can you help me see whether this is a genuine orientation difference or a 'pattern vs life' confusion?"*

---

*"I want to evaluate this organization's practices using BIGCOT. Here's what I know about how it operates..."*

---

*"Help me rewrite this fundraising paragraph using Truthful Speech of Care — without the usual euphemisms about 'impact' and 'change'."*

---

*"We're designing a mutual aid structure. Can you run through the CEFR questions for this design and flag where we might be creating hidden Care Debt?"*

---

*"Something about this 'regenerative' business feels off to me. Can you check whether the RS (Regenerative Surplus) conditions are actually met, or whether this is Harm Dividend in green clothes?"*

---

The AI is not the oracle. It is a thinking partner that has ingested a shared grammar. The analysis it produces is raw material for your own judgment.

**Step 3: Push back**

The grammar is not a formula. Where the analysis doesn't ring true to your experience — push back. Tell the agent what it's missing. Lived experience, grief, and joy are what this framework cannot generate on its own. You bring those; the grammar provides structural vocabulary.

### Path B: Without AI — Solo Practice

You don't need an AI agent to use this grammar. It can be practiced alone or in groups.

**Single-practice exercises:**

**This week: Truthful Speech**
Every time you encounter a euphemism — "human resources," "collateral damage," "free range," "stakeholder value," "strategic realignment" — pause. Ask:
> *Who does what to whom?*

Write it out in plain language. This alone is a form of Gaian practice.

---

**This week: One BIGCOT Dial**
Pick one organization or system you participate in. Set just one BIGCOT dial for it: the **Boundary** dial. Is it currently:
- Diffuse (no edges, anything goes in or out)?
- Membrane (semi-permeable, discerning)?
- Shield (rigid, exclusionary)?

What would it take to move it one notch toward membrane?

---

**This week: Map One Flow**
Think of a system you're part of — your workplace, your neighborhood, your household. Ask:
- Who contributes care here that isn't named or compensated?
- Who absorbs harm here that isn't named?
- Is someone gaining from that harm, or is the mechanism different?

Receiving care does not create a debt of reciprocity. Name exploitation in the conduct of powerful actors, not in the dependency of beings who need care.

That's CEFR in its most basic form.

---

**Group practice: The CEFR Round**

For a meeting, circle, or working group: at the end, take 10 minutes and go around the room. Each person names one thing that flowed as care during this session, and one thing that might have flowed as harm — however small.

This is not conflict escalation. It is care flow auditing as practice.

## What This Is Not

**This is not a new ideology.** The grammar is scaffolding. Your movement keeps its own vocabulary, its own lineage, its own depth of knowledge. Lingua Gaiana offers connection points, not replacements.

**This is not a checklist for moral purity.** The framework specifically includes the "no-hidden-victims epistemology" — which acknowledges that perfect supply chain tracing is impossible, and the practice is honest effort with named limitations, not paralysis or performance.

**This is not AI-dependent.** Path A is faster. Path B is deeper. Both are valid.

**This is not the final word.** Every document in this commons explicitly invites revision. The only restriction is: don't enclose it as proprietary.

**This is not a scientific proof by analogy.** The biological language helps us organize questions; empirical claims need sources and causal claims need testing. Potential means possible futures, not inevitable progress toward care. The commitments to protect, mend and nurture are values, stated openly.

**This is not a ledger of human worth.** No being has to earn moral consideration through usefulness, self-sufficiency or reciprocal contribution. When analyzing care and harm, distinguish need from extraction.

If you've used this grammar in your context and want to leave something in the commons:

## Quick Reference: Entry Questions and Grammar

| Entry Question | Grammar Tool | What It Does |
|---|---|---|
| What is this? | BIGCOT | Reads structural configuration: Boundary, Integrity, Governance, Capability, Orientation, Tempo |
| What is in it? | PCC | Examines material capacity, persistence and possible futures; not a test of moral worth or a biological definition of life |
| How does it change? | CEFR + PMN/VAE | Traces flows of care and harm; names who does what to whom |

| Two Rules | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Truthful Speech of Care | Name who benefits, who pays, how you know. No passive harm. |
| Situated Harm | Who → does what → to whom → when → at what scale. Always. |

## The Frustration This Starts With

> Someone in your movement uses the word "sustainability."
> The corporation you're fighting also uses the word "sustainability."
> How do you tell the difference?

Lingua Gaiana gives you a question: **follow the flows.** Not the narrative — the flows.

Where does the Care Debt accumulate? Who carries the Harm Return? Who collects the Harm Dividend? Is there a regenerative surplus that isn't built on hidden victims?

The grammar doesn't replace judgment. It sharpens it.

**One more question:** What have you actually observed, what are you inferring, and what might change your mind? Use the grammar as a way to discover missing evidence as well as hidden flows.
