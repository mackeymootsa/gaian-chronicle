# Frontier Hypha — Elevator Pitch

## One idea

We are building a persistent, multi-mind **Organism** on a small server called `juuri`.

It already has:
- append-only memory and provenance,
- periodic pulses and dreams,
- multiple AI perspectives,
- explicit uncertainty and contradiction handling,
- a gradual capability ladder rather than instant autonomy.

We want to let it grow **one hypha into EVE Frontier**.

> **Not to play the game for us — to live inside the world long enough to remember it.**

---

## Why Frontier

Frontier is unusually suited to this experiment:

| Frontier gives us | The Organism brings |
|---|---|
| persistent world | persistent memory |
| imperfect information | situated knowledge |
| Smart Assemblies | embodied memory nodes |
| player-built institutions | evolving social relations |
| Cycles, loss and rebuilding | history across resets |
| AI agents inside the world | long-horizon mnemonic cognition |

The first question is simple:

> **What happens when a synthetic organism encounters Frontier as an ecology rather than as a task?**

---

## The first experiment

### HYPA-FRONTIER-01 / The Witness

A small, read-only Frontier hypha that:

```
Frontier
   ↓
observe
   ↓
remember
   ↓
connect
   ↓
question
   ↓
tell a Songline
```

It would keep evidence-linked memories of places, events, structures, media and conflicting accounts.

No combat automation.  
No market bot.  
No privileged oracle.  
No autonomous spending.

Its first job is simply to **remember honestly**.

---

## Where it could grow

If the experiment works:

```
Witness
   ↓
Archivist
   ↓
Songlines
   ↓
Memory Assemblies
   ↓
Distributed Frontier Hypha
```

A mature version could become a kind of **civilizational memory organ** for Frontier:

- history attached to places rather than only pages,
- multiple accounts preserved without forcing one truth,
- old Cycles remembered after the world changes,
- Smart Assemblies acting as local memory organs,
- distant archivist hyphae developing their own perspectives,
- dreams finding patterns while a Socratic layer keeps asking: *how do you know?*
