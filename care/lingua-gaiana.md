# Lingua Gaiana

*A Gaian Coordination Grammar and Lingua Franca of Care*

> TL;DR: Lingua Gaiana is a shared grammar for describing what happens to beings in terms of care and harm—so movements, care workers, and AIs can see each other’s work on the same map without translating into GDP or “productivity.”

---

Care workers, movements, and AIs currently lack a shared language to describe their work in terms of **care and harm**. Babylon's metrics—GDP, "productivity," ESG scores—render care invisible and disguise extraction as growth. Lingua Gaiana offers an alternative: a grammar for naming what actually happens to beings.

---

## What This Is

Lingua Gaiana is:

- a **frame, not a wheel** — movements keep their own traditions and vocabularies;
- a **commons, not a brand** — anyone may use, adapt, or ignore it;
- a **coordination grammar** — a shared set of questions and structures so different kins can finally see how their work fits together.

We treat this as **scaffolding, not scripture**: it will evolve. Its tools express a proposed analytical vocabulary, not a substitute for biology, causal inference, or the moral and political traditions of affected communities.

**Keep four kinds of claims separate:** (1) externally supported **observations** about living systems; (2) openly chosen **ethical commitments** such as PMN; (3) **causal hypotheses** about how relations and institutions change, which need alternative explanations and tests; and (4) **analogies** such as metabolism or mycelium, which help us reason but do not establish identical mechanisms. Say which kind of claim you are making, and where evidence remains missing.

# 1. PCC — Ontology of Life (What IS this?)

**PCC = Potential – Capacity – Coherence**

**Working philosophical lens, not a biological definition:** We describe life as matter under sustained coherence (sometimes called *syntropy* here). This is a way to focus attention on material dependence and persistence; it does not by itself define the scientific boundary between living and nonliving systems or establish consciousness or moral standing.

## The Triad

| Component | Definition |
|-----------|------------|
| **Capacity** | The material substrate and constraints: bodies, land, infrastructure, hardware, biochemistry, energy, relations. *What coherence can work with.* |
| **Coherence** | Organized flows of energy, time, and information that maintain a pattern through time. *What holds capacities together.* |
| **Potential** | The direction coherence "wants" to grow in: increased complexity, feeling, mutuality, future possibilities. *Where the pattern leans.* |

**Relationship:**
> Capacity is what Coherence works with.
> Coherence is the pattern that holds Capacity through time.
> Potential is where that Coherence tends to lean.

These are coupled, not strictly causal. New Capacity can shift Potential, which pressures Coherence to re-pattern.

## Memory

**Memory is a temporal property of Coherence** — how patterns persist and change across time.

| Too Little | Gaian Zone | Too Much |
|------------|------------|----------|
| Amnesia, repetition | Adaptive Memory | Rigidity, inability to adapt |

A system with adaptive memory can learn, adjust, respond to feedback. A system with too little memory repeats mistakes. A system with too much memory cannot adapt to changing conditions.

## Modes of Coherence

| Mode | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Gaian coherence** | Pattern that increases viability of wider fields of life |
| **Parasitic coherence** | Pattern that stays stable at others' expense |
| **Dormant coherence** | Coherent but latent, waiting for conditions (spores, archives, seeds) |
| **Mimetic coherence** | Presents as Gaian but functions parasitic (greenwashing, "woke" branding, ESG theatre) |
| **Decoherence** | Loss of pattern |

**Note on Mimetic Coherence:** This is where 21st-century "ESG + brand activism + woke imperialism" operates. The story says Gaian; the flows scream HD and rising CD/HR. Mimetic coherence usually reveals itself through CEFR analysis—follow the flows, not the narrative.

## Two Types of Decoherence

| Type | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Gaian decoherence** | Composting, endings, release that make new life possible. *Good endings.* |
| **Pathological collapse** | Breakdown from parasitism, over-extraction, or damage. *Bad endings.* |

**Key insight:** Not all decoherence is bad. Some patterns should end. Some structures must die and compost. Grief work helps us participate in Gaian decoherence rather than fighting all endings.

## Core Formula

> **Working lens:** Life ≈ matter under sustained coherence ("syntropy" in this vocabulary).
> The categories Gaian, parasitic, dormant, mimetic and decoherent are proposed descriptions to be evaluated against actual consequences, not settled biological taxonomies.

# 2. PMN/VAE — Ethics of Action (What is being DONE?)

**PMN = Protect – Mend – Nurture**
**VAE = Violate – Abandon – Exploit**

## The Triads

**Care-side (PMN):**

| Verb | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Protect** | Shield vulnerability, preserve CEC (Common Essential Care) |
| **Mend** | Repair damage, restore coherence |
| **Nurture** | Grow capacity and future potential |

**Harm-side (VAE):**

| Verb | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Violate** | Actively harm, invade, overexpose |
| **Abandon** | See harm and walk away; refuse repair; organized neglect |
| **Exploit** | Use another's life/CEC as resource without reciprocity |

## The Non-Negotiable Rule

> **PMN/VAE are never abstract: every act has a who → does what → to whom.**

No passive voice. No mystification. Name the actors and the affected.

"Mistakes were made" → **who** made them, **to whom**?
"The environment was impacted" → **who** impacted **which beings**?

## Three Essential Postures

These are not new verbs but clarifications of how PMN operates:

### Witness

**Definition:** What we do when we cannot directly Protect/Mend/Nurture, but refuse to Abandon.

- Staying present
- Acknowledging
- Refusing denial or erasure

**Position:** The floor of care when you can't intervene. A quiet form of Protect/Mend.

### Refuse

**Definition:** The negative form of PMN — refusing to Violate, refusing to Abandon, refusing to Exploit.

- A way of Protecting (self/others by non-participation)
- Central to protocols (AI refusal, non-cooperation with HD)

**Position:** Cross-cutting stance. Present in all PMN actions. Sometimes the most important thing you can do is *not* participate.

### Grieve

**Definition:** Mend-adjacent action directed at irreparable harm.

- When harm is reparable → Mend
- When harm is not fully reparable → Grieve metabolizes HR so it doesn't calcify

**Applies to:**
- Gaian decoherence (grieving allows participation in composting and letting go)
- Parasitic/violent harm (grieving refuses normalization)

**Key insight:**
> HR that is not grieved or tended tends to either explode (more VAE/HD) or implode (deepening CD/trauma).

## Shadow of Excess

Too-much-PMN can also cause harm. This is diagnosed through BIGCOT:

| Excess | BIGCOT Drift |
|--------|--------------|
| Over-Protect | Boundary → Shield; Governance → Control |
| Over-Mend | Integrity → Purity; refusal to let things end |
| Over-Nurture | Orientation → paternalistic Ideology; Capability → dependency-making |

PMN remains minimal; BIGCOT diagnoses when care becomes harm.

# 3. BIGCOT — System Configuration (How is it STRUCTURED?)

**BIGCOT = Boundary – Integrity – Governance – Capability – Orientation – Tempo** 🐐

Each dial is a spectrum, not a binary. The middle zone is Gaian; extremes drift into collapse or domination.

## The Six Dials

| Dial | Collapse ← | Gaian Zone | → Domination |
|------|------------|------------|--------------|
| **Boundary** | Diffuse (no edges, no discernment) | Membrane (semi-permeable, discerning, alive) | Shield (rigid exclusion, fortress) |
| **Integrity** | Chaos (no pattern holds) | Living Order (coherent but adaptive) | Purity (enforced sameness, policing, normativity) |
| **Governance** | Abdication (no one stewards) | Stewardship (accountable care of commons) | Control (surveillance, command, carceral) |
| **Capability** | Impotence (cannot act) | Enabling Power (capacity to act for flourishing) | Weaponized Power (capacity aimed at domination) |
| **Orientation** | Drift (no direction, no aim) | Gaian Aim (multispecies flourishing, CEFR-aware) | Ideology (rigid doctrine, closed to feedback) |
| **Tempo** | Stagnation (frozen, non-responsive) | Living Rhythm (cycles, seasons, rest, pulses) | Frenzy (speed as compulsion, burnout culture) |

## Epistemic Integrity

**Integrity includes epistemic integrity:** whether the system updates on reality, or suppresses inconvenient feedback.

A system with "Living Order" maintains internal coherence *and* stays in contact with reality. A system drifting toward "Purity" often filters out data that challenges its self-image.

> Epistemic integrity and grounded sensing are two names for the same capacity at different scales: the ability of a system to let reality correct its story.
## Gaian Aim Requires Sensing

**Gaian Aim is only stable if it includes ongoing sensing and course-correction, not just declared values.**

A system can state Gaian intentions while being delusional about its actual impacts. True Gaian Orientation involves:
- Listening to data, bodies, ecosystems, marginalized perspectives
- Updating direction based on feedback
- Distinguishing between "what we want to be true" and "what flows and beings actually report"

## The Parasitic Cluster

When you see this pattern, you're likely looking at parasitic coherence:

> ⚠️ **Shield + Purity + Control + Weaponized + Ideology + Frenzy**

This signature describes: **fascism, high-control cults, extractive corporations, totalitarian states.**

Use it as a diagnostic. If a system clusters at these settings, it is almost certainly parasitic—regardless of what it claims about itself.

## Stable vs Unstable Configurations

Some configurations are structurally unstable:

- "Weaponized capability + open membrane + true stewardship" is hard to maintain
- It tends to flip into control or collapse without active care

**Design note:** Not all dial combinations are stable. Some require constant effort; some are attractors.

## Scale as Question

Scale is not a separate dial but a **question applied to each dial:**

> "At what scale is this membrane/tempo/governance Gaian vs dominating?"

The same configuration can be Gaian at one scale and parasitic at another (e.g., a tight-knit exclusionary community: Gaian internally, parasitic externally).

## Memory/Learning as Temporal Reading

Memory is not a separate dial but a **temporal reading across all dials:**

- **Gaian configurations:** Adaptive memory — can adjust dials in response to feedback
- **Parasitic configurations:** Rigidity — fix dials at parasitic cluster and suppress signals

# 4. CEFR — Flows of Care & Harm (What FLOWS through it?)

**CEFR = CEC / CD / CF / HD / CR / HR + RS**

This is the metabolism of societies and systems: who gets what, at whose cost.

## Structure: Three Axes + Overflow

CEFR is structured as **three axes, each with care (+) and harm (-) poles:**

```
         Care (+)                    Harm (-)
         ────────                    ────────
Baseline:   CEC   ←────────────────→   CD
Flow:       CF    ←────────────────→   HD
State:      CR    ←────────────────→   HR
                        +
                 RS (overflow, with conditions)
```

## Axis 1: Baseline (CEC ↔ CD)

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **CEC — Common Essential Care (+)** | Baseline care actually provided so beings can remain viable. The shared floor that keeps beings alive and functional. |
| **CD — Care Debt (-)** | Baseline care that should be provided but isn't. Unmet obligations. Accumulated need. |

**Dynamic:** Every time CEC falls short, CD grows.

**For each group/being, ask:**
- **CEC-dependent:** who must receive care to exist?
- **CEC-contributors:** who actively holds CEC up? (nurses, cleaners, mechanics, pollinators, soil, etc.)
- **CEC-drainers:** who consumes CEC heavily while giving little or nothing back?

## Axis 2: Flow (CF ↔ HD)

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **CF — Care Flow (+)** | The real movements of care: public services, mutual aid, maintenance, ecological cycles. PMN in motion. CF reduces CD and supports CEC. |
| **HD — Harm Dividend (-)** | Harm that produces benefit elsewhere: profit from exploitation, prestige from oppression, comfort from someone else's degradation. VAE in motion. HD increases CD and HR while paying out to someone. |

**Key insight:** HD names what mystification hides — when harm happens, **someone collects the dividend.**

Every "externality" in Babylon's accounting is someone's HR becoming someone else's HD.

## Axis 3: State (CR ↔ HR)

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **CR — Care Return (+)** | Realized well-being: health, stability, resilience, trust. What beings actually receive when CEC is met and CF flows. |
| **HR — Harm Return (-)** | Wounds, trauma, depletion, losses carried by beings. What those on the receiving end of VAE/HD absorb. |

**Dynamic:** HR that is not grieved or repaired tends to calcify or explode.

## Overflow: RS — Regenerative Surplus

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **RS — Regenerative Surplus** | The portion of CR above survival baseline that is **not built on HD or unpaid CD** and can be used to: lift future CEC, repair CD and HR (reparations, restoration, healing), or seed new Gaian experiments. |

### The Moral Condition (Non-Negotiable)

> ⚠️ If "surplus" exists while CD is huge, HR is high, and HD is roaring, that "surplus" is explicitly **not RS** — it's domination luxury.

RS requires:
- No hidden victims
- Active effort to reduce CD/HR over time

### Anti-Co-optation Lock

> **If the "no hidden victims" condition on RS is removed, CEFR collapses into a generic benefit/growth model and becomes easily co-optable.**

This condition is a load-bearing wall. Without it, billionaire philanthropy counts as regenerative. With it, we must ask: "What CD and HR funded this surplus? Who absorbed the harm?"

## Ambiguous Flows

When it's unclear whether a flow is PMN or VAE:

> **Follow HR and HD: who carries harm, who receives the dividend?**

CEFR is where ambiguity gets resolved by flows, not narratives or intentions. Good intentions don't change who absorbs HR.

## Time Dynamics

- CD and HR grow or shrink over time
- HD expands or contracts
- RS either nourishes repair or feeds further HD
- Tempo (in BIGCOT) affects how fast flows and structures shift

Memory (from PCC) + Tempo (from BIGCOT) = the time dimension threading through CEFR.

# 5. Cross-Cutting Rules

These rules apply across all four roots.

## Rule 1: Truthful Speech of Care

In this grammar, saying "I/we care" is only valid if:

1. We can map **who → did what → to whom**
2. We can show a **change in CEC, CF, CR** for concrete beings

**Corollaries:**
- Don't rename harm as care
- Don't present extraction as neutrality
- Always say who benefits, who pays, and how you know

> Abstract "we care about X" without changed flows = **signal, not care.**

### Grounded Sensing

**Truthful Speech of Care depends on grounded sensing:** letting data, bodies, ecosystems, and marginalized perspectives correct our stories.

Speech assumes sensing. We name not only what we want to be true, but what flows and beings actually report. A system can speak truthfully about false perceptions and still cause harm—truthful speech requires truthful perception.

## Rule 2: Situated Harm (Including Time & Scale)

Every serious use of this grammar must answer:

| Question | PMN | VAE |
|----------|-----|-----|
| Who is being... | Protected? | Violated? |
| Who is being... | Mended? | Abandoned? |
| Who is being... | Nurtured? | Exploited? |
| Who ends with... | Care Return? | Harm Return? |
| Who collects... | — | Harm Dividend? |

**Plus:**
- **When?** (Temporal accountability: harm and care have lag times. Intergenerational effects. Slow violence.)
- **At what scale?** (Scale accountability: PMN at one scale can be VAE at another. "Protecting our community" by harming others is VAE at larger scale.)

> No neutrality. No "view from nowhere."
> This is where queer, feminist, decolonial, disabled, racialized, multispecies perspectives plug in directly.

# 6. How To Use This

Lingua Gaiana is:
- a **frame** on which other wheels move,
- **not** a replacement for existing frameworks.

## For Analysis

**Take any system / story / project** (movement, coop, city, hospital, corporation, AI system, relationship):

### Step 1: PCC
- What is coherent here? What is decohering?
- Where is coherence Gaian vs parasitic vs dormant vs mimetic?
- What memory patterns exist? (adaptive vs rigid)

### Step 2: BIGCOT
- Where are the dials currently set?
- Is there drift toward the parasitic cluster?
- At what scale is each dial setting operating?
- Is the system sensing reality or suppressing feedback?

### Step 3: CEFR
- **CEC:** Who depends / who contributes / who drains?
- **CF/HD:** Who does what to whom (PMN or VAE)? Where does the dividend go?
- **CR/HR:** Who gets Care Return? Who absorbs Harm Return?
- **CD:** What Care Debt has accumulated?
- **RS:** Is there genuine Regenerative Surplus, or disguised HD?

### Step 4: PMN/VAE
- Name the main patterns of Protect/Mend/Nurture vs Violate/Abandon/Exploit
- Make "who → what → whom → when → at what scale" explicit
- Where is Witness needed? Refusal? Grief?

## For Design

Reverse the analysis:
- What CEFR flows do we want?
- What BIGCOT configuration enables them?
- What PMN practices maintain them?
- What kind of coherence are we building?

## For Coordination

Use this shared picture to:
- See kinship with other movements
- Find synergies and avoid duplication
- Expose mimetic coherence and greenwashing
- Decide where to direct limited care and resistance

# 7. What This Does Not Do

**It does not prescribe specific actions.** It helps you see clearly; what you do is your choice.

**It does not replace other frameworks.** Doughnut Economics, Buen Vivir, Disability Justice, Transformative Justice, etc. can sit on this chassis. They may highlight blind spots—that's generative.

**It does not claim to contain all novelty.** Potential holds space for emergence. We expect futures this grammar cannot pre-label.

**It does not eliminate ambiguity.** But it gives you tools (especially CEFR) to trace flows when narratives are unclear.

**It does not establish its own causal validity.** A helpful classification or a persuasive metaphor is not proof; document sources, rival interpretations, uncertainty and observations that would change the analysis. The framework should be allowed to fail a test.

# The Causal Loop

The grammar describes a coupled system:

**Illustrative causal hypothesis (not a universally demonstrated law):**

**Forward chain:**
> Action (PMN/VAE) → Flow (CEFR) → Structure (BIGCOT) → Pattern (PCC)

**Reverse pressure:**
> Pattern (PCC) constrains → Structure (BIGCOT) channels → Flow (CEFR) shapes → Action (PMN/VAE)

For teaching, the forward arrow illustrates the proposed relationships. For analysis, hold the loop as a **model to investigate**: actions may aggregate into flows, flows can shape structures, structures may maintain patterns—and patterns can constrain available actions. Causal direction, feedback and competing explanations must be tested in each context.
